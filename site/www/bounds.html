The best packing and the proven floor

For each n two numbers are known: the side of the best packing anyone has found, and a floor that a proof says no packing can beat. Where they meet, s(n) is settled; elsewhere the truth lies in the gap, and for most n the gap has barely moved in forty years. Floors are collected for n ≤ 100.
