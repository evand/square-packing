Packings Row by Row

Row k holds every count n from (k − 1)² + 1 to k², right-aligned, so the column counted from the right is the deficit c = k² − n. Each Lean-checked construction gives one packing per row: k² − c squares in a square of side less than k. Its cell shows that packing in miniature, and every cell to its left follows by deleting squares. Hover a cell to see it larger; click to open the packing, then step to the next one in its series.

Fang's gluing

k² − k + 1 + p + 2q in side < k, k ≥ 15, 16p + 21q ≤ k − 15

Kevin Fang's theorem, after Arslanov, Mustafin and Shangitbayev: a base piece of side 15 to 30, then 17 × 16 and 22 × 21 rectangles (257 and 443 squares) glued on, each with aligned blocks. Linear: about k − 1 − 2(k − 15)/21. Best here for 15 ≤ k ≤ 39. Lean: FangFinal.theorem2.

Ryu's L-shape

c*(k) ≤ 8⌈√(k − 4)⌉ − 1, k ≥ 6

Sungjoon Ryu's Theorem 1.4: an axis grid of side k − b and two bands of rows of b squares, tilted just past 2 arctan(1/b), so each row is a little narrower than b. The band ends are left empty. Lean: RyuBound.packs_sub_8sqrt.

The filled L-shape

c*(k) ≤ 4⌈√(2k − 7)⌉ − 1, k ≥ 7

Ryu's L-shape with b = ⌈√(2k − 7)⌉ and axis squares in the four band ends (green in the viewer): 2b of them in all. Best here from k = 40 to about 7.4 · 10⁵. As far as we know a new bound. Lean: RyuLayers.packs_sub_4sqrt2_layers.

Reading the triangle

  • A construction's cell is the packing its theorem gives: in row k, column c, it packs k² − c squares in side < k, so c*(k) ≤ c − 1. The cells to its left are the same packing with squares deleted.
  • Green cells are where side k is proved necessary: the grid families s(k² − c) = k for c ≤ 4 (for c = 3 from k = 6, c = 4 from k = 5), and every n ≤ 324 whose lower bound has reached k (some of those proofs are from 2026 and unrefereed). Open cells are where neither a packing below side k nor such a proof is known.
  • The ring is the best known packing below side k by any method, from the jlevy/squares register and the packings reported on its issues (some not yet accepted there), up to k = 40. Those are search results and are not drawn here. Beyond k = 40 the constructions are all that is known.
  • Drawings are floating point. The exact statements are the Lean theorems; search/ryu/explorer_check.mjs re-checks every drawing on this page (count equal to the theorem's, side < k, no overlaps) for k ≤ 400.
  • Not drawn: the staircase bound c*(k) < 18.9 k2/5 + 35.7 k1/5 + 122, which starts at k = 3 · 10⁵ (see the write-up).
