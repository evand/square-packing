an open problem you can hold in your hands

How small a square can hold n unit squares?

Call the answer s(n). For a perfect square number of squares the answer is obvious. For most other n nobody knows: we only know the best packing anyone has found, and a proven floor below which no packing can go. This site collects the record packings (from Erich Friedman's and David Ellsworth's catalogue), lets you take them apart, and walks through how the few exact results are proved.
