## Outcomes

* **The fully pinned Bentz leaf is at 12, exactly, and no LP was needed to see it.**  With `P₀` =
  Bentz's 16 points, four corner squares holding `{a_i, b_i}` force (by `K + u = 4`) every other
  square to hold exactly one of `{c_j, d_j}`; that leaf deletes 12 % of the corner instance's
  poses — and none of the 162 poses of the certified `PGCORN` measure, whose twelve pattern masses
  are all `1 − O(1e-8)`.  With `μ(R_π) <= μ(R_p) <= 1` the leaf is bracketed
  **`11.999999926 <= value <= 12`** on any pose set (`BENTZ.md` §0).  The tree it sits in: 93
  realisable patterns (never 5, never 0), 86,403 leaves, 10,945 up to `D4`, 43 classes at the
  corner-pattern level, and the `(AB)⁴` class *is* this leaf.  Pattern branching on a finite point
  set has zero power at `t = 4`; its integral kill (`s(4) = 2` on the interior) is invisible to the LP.
* **Axis-parallel is trivial: exactly 9.**  Restricting the corner `k = 4` instance to `θ ≡ 0`
  converges in 71 s to an *integral* nine-square packing, and `{1,2,3}²` is a closed cover for
  axis-parallel squares, so `9` is a theorem.  **All three missing units are rotation.**  With
  `|θ mod 90°| <= 1.0000034°` the certified value is **`11.599390`** (`M = 1` exact, regions OK):
  **at least `2.6` of the `3` is bought by tilts under one degree** — the item-3 family of
  `HONEST.md` (grid squares nudged `1e-3` and tilted a fraction of a degree, dodging every point on
  the grid lines at a bounding-box cost of `O(θ)`).
* **Corner `k = 3` does not close either**: certified **`12.027273`** (`M < 1` exact, corner masses
  `0.9991, 0.9991, 0.9991, 0` — the `1e-3` boundary tie-break as for `PGCORN`), LP `12.038` and
  still rising at the 4 h stop.  So at `t = 4` the corner branch reads pure `12.17` → `k = 3`
  `>= 12.03` → `k = 4` `12.000`, and a corner-count tree is dead alongside the pattern tree.  The
  only count leaves not yet shown `>= 12` are the level-2 slot leaves (`T4LEAF.md`: `11.92`, rising,
  pose-set-starved).
* **The missing unit is localised, in integers, on the optimum's own support.**  The largest
  pairwise-disjoint subfamily of the 162 poses has **11** members; `rankdiag` independently gives
  `α(G) = 11`, gap `1.000000` exactly, with no violated C5/C7/antihole/5-wheel.  Each group of four
  patterns is realisable on its own; **the eight non-corner singletons together admit only seven**.
  The statement a proof needs is therefore a rank-8 geometric fact — *with four corner squares
  holding `{a_i, b_i}`, at most seven further squares can each contain one of the eight points
  `c_j, d_j`* — true integrally (one wall square per wall between two pinned corners, then
  `s(4) = 2` on the interior) and inexpressible by any single-square (degree-1) inequality.

## Reading

The degree-1 programme for `n = 12` at the container is now closed in every direction it can be
taken: pure (`12.27`), polygons (`12.17`), chord (0), sound cliques (`ALLMEET.md`), corner counts
(`k = 3, 4` both `>= 12`), pattern counts (exactly 12).  What all of them relax is the same thing,
and today's runs say what it is: not the tiling — axis-parallel squares are trivially `<= 9` — but
the tiling **infinitesimally tilted**, where a square dodges the grid-line points at first-order
cost and the LP averages over a continuum of such near-squares.  The integrality gap of exactly one
square lives on eight labelled squares confined to compact pose regions, and the LP cannot see it
because it is a pairwise statement (disjointness of eight specific squares), not a single-square one.

That makes the next step mathematics, not another branch tree.  Two shapes are visible:

1. **Analytic / local.**  Prove the rank-8 statement directly.  Every square in it is within a
   small pose neighbourhood of its tile and tilted by under a degree (that is where `>= 2.6` of the
   `3` lives), so the natural tool is a linearised rigidity argument around the tiling — first-order
   disjointness constraints along rows and columns, with the classical `"> 1"` strictness lemmas
   (Nagamochi, Bentz's Corollary 7) supplying what closed semantics loses.  The machine's job would
   then be the leaf checks outside the neighbourhood, where margins are positive and the existing
   tools work.
2. **Degree-2.**  A pairwise (Sherali–Adams level-2) relaxation on the twelve *labelled* squares
   of the fully pinned leaf: joint distributions per pair supported on disjoint poses.  The dual is a
   cover with pair terms; whether pair terms can be credited over the continuum is an open design
   question, and nothing in the repo does it.

Both are research.  Neither is "finish a couple of things".

## Decisions

- No more count or pattern branch trees at `t = 4`; the corner, slot and incidence levels are
  measured and recorded.  V1 stays unbuilt.  The packing-side instruments are done.
- The `n = 12` negative result is worth a self-contained note (what every degree-1 family does at
  `t = 4`, the axis-parallel `9`, the sub-degree-tilt `>= 2.6`, the rank-8 localisation) — it is the
  precise statement of why the problem is hard, and it is new.
- `s(11)` is the hedge: the same rotation-carried gap (`0.06` in side against Trump's tilted
  packing), all of it `t < 4` work with the sweep verifier; the first question is the anatomy of the
  mass-11 fractional packing at `t ≈ 3.82–3.85`.
