# n = 11: where does the cover LP cross 11?  (and a new lower bound for s(11))

Code: `search/tighten.py` with the new `--n` option (default 12; it only changes the weight
threshold: the `n` handed to the verifier and the "not a certificate" test), driven from a scratch
directory with the verifier capped at 2–4 threads (shared machine, ≤ 8 threads in total).
Everything below is ~1 h 50 min of wall clock on 26 Aug 2026; certificates and logs are in the
gitignored `runs/n11_*`.  Conventions are exactly those of the `n = 12` work: container `[0,t]²`,
**closed** unit squares, D4-symmetric point sets, exact `i128` verifier (`verify CERT 11 N T 0`)
at `N = 6000` and `N = 12000`.

**Outcome.**

* **New lower bound (rigorous): `s(11) ≥ 3040/797 = 3.8143036`**, certificate
  `runs/n11_H3985.txt` (680 points, total weight 10.8146708 < 11), accepted by the exact verifier
  at `N = 6000` and `N = 12000` with `n = 11`, and by the independent `xcheck.py --all --n 11`
  (`runs/n11_H3985_xcheck.txt`: VERIFIED, minimum 5000021/5000000 = 1.0000042 at bin k = 0, the same
  number the Rust verifier reports).  The previous record is Stromquist's `2 + 4/√5 = 3.788854`
  (a 10-point unavoidable set plus a case split), so this is `+0.0254`, and it is a *pure* LP
  certificate: no case analysis.  Two more certificates: `19/5 = 3.8` (960 points, 10.6913) and
  `3040/801 = 3.79526` (988 points, 10.8445).
* **The crossing of the LP is at `t* ≈ 3.815–3.817`** — about **0.06 below** Trump's
  `3.877083` (conjectured optimal).  So the pure weighted-unavoidable-set method cannot reach
  the conjectured value for `n = 11`; the required gap is twice the one at `n = 12`
  (`≈ 0.03`, `search/CEILING.md`, `search/TIGHTEN.md`).  Above the crossing the LP settles
  at **exactly 11** (`11.000022 = 11·(1+2·10⁻⁶)`, the LP margin) with a dual packing measure of
  total mass exactly 11 whose D4-symmetrised coverage is `≤ 1` on the pricing grid — i.e. a
  clean fractional packing of mass 11 appears at `t ≈ 3.816` and the method is dead from there.

## Procedure

1. **Seeds.**  A cover at container `t` is a cover at any `t' < t` after rescaling (integer
   coordinates fixed, denominator `D` changed: `--Dp`).  Fixed-point re-optimisation (`reopt`,
   no column generation) of the shipped `n = 12` certificates rescaled to `3.79–3.875` never gets
   below `11.59` (table below): the 224-point set is tuned to the `n = 12` structure near 3.93.
   The shipped uniform sets are better seeds — `s12_uniform_5of56` is `11.2` at every container
   tried (its uniform weights are already LP-optimal on those 56 points).
2. **Column generation** (`--colgen k --cg-want 150`, exact reduced-cost pricing on a 0.01 grid,
   verifier witnesses as cuts, `N = 6000`, `topk = 6`).  Two exploratory runs from the 56-point
   seed (`G85` at 3.85, `G80` at 3.80, colgen every round, killed after 25 min) showed that the
   LP value *during* colgen is not trustworthy: the row set is far from complete (up to 15k
   violated placements per round), and a cut-only re-optimisation of the current *support*
   (`R80`: 11.054 at 3.80 from the 27-orbit support of `G80` whose LP said 10.42) lands above
   the colgen value.  So the production runs (`H*`, `J*`) do 10–12 colgen rounds and then keep
   the **full column set** (800–1200 orbits) and iterate cut-only until the probe is clean, which
   is what `tighten()` does by itself; `finalize` then exports at `W = 10⁷` and re-verifies at
   `N = 6000` and `12000`.  Seeds: `n11_seed_union.txt` (56 uniform points ∪ the `G85`/`G80`
   supports, 840 points, `K = 15200`) for `H*`; `n11_seed_union2.txt` (that ∪ the `H*` supports,
   3384 points) for `J*`.  Runs converge (0 violated placements) in 25–50 min at 2–3 threads.
3. **Bracket.**  `D = 3985, 3984, 3983, 3982, 3980, 3975, 3965` around the transition.

**Crossing bracket.**  `t_lo = 3040/797 = 3.81430` (rigorous: exact-verified certificate of
weight 10.8147 < 11).  `t_hi ≈ 3.8153` (heuristic: at `D = 3984` the LP with frozen columns is at `11.017` with
rows still incomplete, and at `D = 3983, 3982, 3980, 3975` it sits at `11.000022`; the
remaining cuts can only raise these values; the union seed contained the supports of the successful runs at
3.795–3.814, so these column sets are not obviously worse than those).  Width of the bracket
`≈ 0.001` (`3.81430 ≤ t* ≲ 3.81526`), below the requested 0.005.  `runs/n11_H3985_critical.txt`:
`scale_to_critical.py --n 11 --N 6000` on the 3.8143 certificate returns `D = 3985`, i.e. the file
is already at its critical container (the `2·10⁻⁶` LP margin leaves nothing to scale).

**What the LP does above the crossing.**  At every container from 3.8162 up (and already at 3.8153) the colgen LP drops
to 10.1–10.9 while rows are incomplete and then returns to `11.000022 = 11·(1 + m_lp)`
exactly, with the pricing step reporting a maximal symmetrised dual coverage of exactly `1.0000`
(no column with negative reduced cost on the 0.01 grid).  The dual is then a packing measure
of mass exactly 11 with coverage `≤ 1` on the grid: a fractional packing of 11 unit squares
appears at `t ≈ 3.816`.  Below the crossing (`H3985`, 3.8143) the same procedure converges to
10.81 with the probe clean, so the transition is sharp — a cliff of the kind `TIGHTEN.md`
describes for `n = 12` at 3.9496, not a slow drift.  What that mass-11 fractional packing looks
like geometrically was not extracted (it would be the obstruction to prove anything better by
weights alone, and the starting point for a case split in Stromquist's style).

**Follow-up 2026-09-13 (`search/N11_ANATOMY.md`).**  It was extracted, and it is four poses: the
four corner squares at mass exactly 1, one unit of mass per wall split over two overlapping
axis-parallel squares, and three units in the interior (`4 + 4 + 3 = 11`), with `alpha = 10` and an
integrality gap of exactly `1`.  The top end of the bracket is now rigorous:
`nu_f(96/25) >= 11` exactly, so **`COVER(t) >= 11` for every `t >= 3.84`** and the ceiling of the
pure method for `n = 11` is `3.814304 <= s*(11) <= 3.84`.  `COVER(3800/987) <= 11.1162388`
(exact-verified at `N = 6000` and `N = 12000`) replaces the `11.2608` row of the table above.

## Rigorous vs heuristic

* **Rigorous** (exact integer arithmetic end to end, `verify` at `N = 6000` and `N = 12000` with
  `n = 11`, outputs in `runs/n11_H*_verify.txt`): the three certificates `runs/n11_H3985.txt`
  (`s(11) ≥ 3040/797`), `runs/n11_H4000.txt` (`s(11) ≥ 19/5`), `runs/n11_H4005.txt`
  (`s(11) ≥ 3040/801`); every `min`/`total` entry in the table; and the upper bounds
  `COVER(3.8500) ≤ 11.2608` (`runs/n11_3.85_R85.txt`), `COVER(3.8) ≤ 10.6913`.  The rigour
  rests on the verifier's exactness, exactly as for `n = 12` (`certificates/FORMAT.md`,
  `VERIFICATION.md`); `xcheck.py --all --n 11` at `N = 6000` accepts the best file with the identical minimum.
* **Heuristic**: every "LP" column entry; the location `t_hi ≈ 3.8153` of the crossing (a
  lower bound on the crossing would need a rigorous fractional packing as in `CEILING.md`'s `L(s)`,
  which was not built here); the claim that the point sets found are near-optimal.  In
  particular the certificates are certainly not the sparsest possible (no `sparsify` pass — time).

## Reproduce

```sh
cd verify && cargo build --release && cd ..
# fixed-point baselines (seconds each)
python3 search/tighten.py scan certificates/s12_lower_3.931795_sparse.txt F --Dps 2036,2047,2063,2026,2069,2023 --n 11
python3 search/tighten.py scan certificates/s12_uniform_5of56_3.852.txt U56 --Dps 3948,3969,4000,3928,3921 --n 11
# column generation, then cut-only convergence on the full column set (25-50 min each)
python3 search/tighten.py reopt runs/n11_seed_union.txt  H4005 --Dp 4005 --colgen 10 --cg-want 150 --n 11 --out runs/n11_H4005.txt
python3 search/tighten.py reopt runs/n11_seed_union.txt  H4000 --Dp 4000 --colgen 10 --cg-want 150 --n 11 --out runs/n11_H4000.txt
python3 search/tighten.py reopt runs/n11_seed_union.txt  H3985 --Dp 3985 --colgen 10 --cg-want 150 --n 11 --out runs/n11_H3985.txt
python3 search/tighten.py reopt runs/n11_seed_union2.txt J3983 --Dp 3983 --colgen 12 --cg-want 150 --n 11 --out runs/n11_J3983.txt
# checks
verify/target/release/verify runs/n11_H3985.txt 11 6000 8 0
verify/target/release/verify runs/n11_H3985.txt 11 12000 8 0
python3 xcheck.py runs/n11_H3985.txt 6000 --all --n 11
python3 search/scale_to_critical.py runs/n11_H3985.txt --n 11 --N 6000
```

The seeds `runs/n11_seed_union*.txt` are unions of run supports (weights only matter for the
lattice warm start); the runs were done at 2–3 verifier threads under load, and the colgen
pricing is deterministic given the seed and HiGHS's pinned `random_seed`, but the `G*` exploratory
runs were stopped by hand so the seeds themselves are not reproducible from a command line.
The `H4005` certificate was also re-verified independently of the driver
(`runs/n11_H4005_verify.txt`).
