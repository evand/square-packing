# Review, 2026-09-08: depth is not the lever, cliques are

**State on entry.**  `s(12) >= 3.968616` certified.  At `t = 4` the family (corner branch + slot
branch + anchor cliques + chord lemma) left the hardest slot leaf `01010101` at 11.745 (cliques) /
11.920 (no cliques), rising under refinement; the corner leaf alone at 11.996; 4213 leaves in the
tree; cover-side 13–27 h per leaf; the sweep verifier unable to verify at margin 0
(`notes/review-2026-09-07.md`).  Verdict then: not a proof in days; whether it is a proof at all
turns on depth.  Nothing on the table could prove `s(12) = 4` in a day, and nothing found today
changes that — but the picture of *what* is missing changed.

**Three tasks launched in parallel** (`tasks/depth-test`, `tasks/clique-lever`, `tasks/verify-speed`;
the two experiments are packing-side and never call the verifier, so the speedup did not gate them).

## Outcomes

* **Depth test** (`search/DEPTH.md`): **drop per level = 0, exactly, as a theorem.**  Leaf
  `01010101` is C4-invariant and C4 cycles the four interior quadrants, so the C4-average of any
  feasible parent measure is feasible for the balanced quadrant child with the same mass; the
  balanced child equals the parent (11.804, all cap duals 0).  Every unbalanced child collapses to
  an integral packing of `8 + #non-empty quadrants` squares, certified exactly (11, 11, 11, 10 ×5).
  The whole fractional excess is the crowding of the four central grid poses around `(2,2)`, which
  only the symmetric child keeps.  Two corrections: the quadrant capacity is 3, not 2 (proved; 2 is
  only heuristic evidence); and **T4LEAF's `kmax` values are unreliable at the 0.1 level** — the
  exact scan finds an anchor clique of mass 1.166 on the run's own measure where the separator
  reported 1.000, so 11.745 was an overestimate of the anchor-clique leaf value.
* **Clique lever** (`search/CLIQUELEVER.md`, `search/cliquelever.py`): **cliques are the lever.**
  The full clique LP (every clique of the closed-intersection graph enforced, exact B&B separator,
  every B&B complete) on the same leaf: **11.303** (exactly certified), **11.321** after a pricing
  stage with the loop fully terminated (`QSTAB(P_1) = 317/28`).  Corner leaf `k = 4` alone:
  **11.674** — 0.33 below 12 with no slot branch.  General cliques are worth 0.44 over the anchor
  family (0.61 over pure); the anchor family captured about a quarter of the excess (median 30 %
  per clique).  Column generation brings back +0.02, not the value: the columns the pricer wants are
  the ones the cliques forbid.  **Shape of every violated clique**: the point clique of a wall-square
  corner `p` (a point on `x, y ∈ {1,2,3}`, mass ≈ 1) plus a *fan* of 30–45 tilted interior squares
  at 12–16 angles that meet every square through `p`, carrying 0.33–0.57.  The fan has no common
  segment, which is why one-segment anchors `K(p, A)` miss most of it.
* **Verifier speed** (`search/VERIFYSPEED.md`, merged at session end; `./verify.sh` exit 0 with all 24 verdicts unchanged): incremental anchor credit
  5.7–13.2× per bin, D4 reduction with an anchor block 2.41× on bins, dynamic scheduling for
  balance; bit-identical outputs (`tests/bitid.sh`), 172 rejection tests (was 138).  `1110` probe
  1699 s → 417 s at 3 threads; `k = 4` probe ≈ 18 h → 39 min (~28×).  Cover-side leaves at
  `t = 3.98` are now ~1 h/round; the 3.98 consolation certificate is cheap whenever wanted.

## Reading

The family's blocker was never depth; it is that the certifiable clique family sees ~30 % of the
non-Helly excess.  With the full clique family the corner leaf alone is at 11.67, so the slot tree
(and its 4213 leaves) may be unnecessary — the proof shape would be *one* certificate (possibly per
corner leaf) with a rich enough clique family, plus zero-margin verification.  Whether pure QSTAB
at `t = 4` (no branch at all) is below 12 is unmeasured and is the cheapest decisive number left.

## Decisions

- Depth branching on symmetric leaves is dead (theorem); do not build the interior tree.
- The certifiable clique family is the main thread: characterize the fan cliques the B&B found,
  define a verifier-checkable family that contains them (a union of segment anchors sharing `p`?
  "meets every square through `p`"?), prove clique-ness, measure recovery of the 0.44 with
  `cliquelever.py` restricted to the family.
- Measure pure QSTAB and the corner leaves `k = 0..3` under QSTAB at `t = 4` before any more tree work.
- Re-run T4LEAF-style anchor-clique numbers only with the exact scan's witnesses fed back
  (`--cq-load`); the separator's `kmax` is not a stopping test.
