## The thesis (Evan, 10-14) -- read before planning
Local search at current budgets has mined the frontier (post-#593).  The out-of-place cells on jlevy's triangle (104,
109, the 45-degree patterns at offset m = n - (k-1)^2 <= 9 on each row, the plateaus) are where the patterns break, and

Mondrian / machete.  Where a Mondrian is the record it is a not-yet-relaxed variant; relaxing after construction is the
unsolved trick (harden rebuilds Ostashev's #575 176 exactly, but never goes below a record).
