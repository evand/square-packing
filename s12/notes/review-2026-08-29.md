# Review, 2026-08-29: where the s(12) project stands and what comes next

**State.** Pure method finished (`3.968616`, ceiling ≈ 3.975–3.98).  Corner branching at 3.98:
`k = 0, 1, 2` certified; `1110` stopped at round 51 (obj 11.9673, +0.0001/round, probe 0.94, 2 h per
LP solve, 40 GB); `1111` at `12.000 ± 0.001` with a 1.49-mass clique in its dual.  Clique cuts on
fixed poses: −0.14 / −0.09 / −0.31 at 3.98 / 3.99 / 4.0, unconverged.

**Reading.** Near 4 the LP excess grows ~0.1 per 0.01 of `t`, so the remaining 0.03 in `t` is
0.3–0.5 in relaxation mass — the same order as the measured clique gains.  Cliques are the right
lever; whether they are enough is the first question (task A).  Separately, a certificate at
`t = 4` must be verified at zero-margin poses, which no erosion verifier can do; that is the
genuinely unbuilt piece of mathematics (task E), and it can be developed on cases with known
answers (s(15), s(13)) and one open one with deficit 1 (s(45)).

**Decisions.**
- Explore on the packing side, certify on the cover side.
- Cliques must be pairwise *closed*-intersecting (touching counts); `clique_check.py`'s strict test
  is conservative for certificates and unsound for ceilings.
- Box cliques (unions of pose boxes, pairwise core intersection) instead of `K(p, A)`: no new lemma.
- LP speed is second overall and first on the cover side; its benchmark runs alone in the heavy slot.
- No more corner-leaf runs with the current loop; no more plus-region packing; no SDP.

**Schedule.** Phase 1 (parallel): A, B, C-benchmark, E.  Phase 2: D, then `k = 4` + cliques on the
cover side, then re-run `1110`.  Phase 3: F if needed; the limit.
