# P010 (gen 1): blocks at two angles, chains and composition
Parents: P004 (gen 0: block + secondary block; count-driven polyline bands beat literal lattice blocks) and P006 (open
design).  Parent program: /home/evand/math/square-packing/public/search/packer/runs/evolve2/gen0/P004/prog_12.py (you may start from it).  Targets: n = 234-240, scored by the softmin.

Vocabulary: one large rotated block plus a smaller block at a different angle tucked into the corner it leaves; or a
chain of blocks at 45, ~40 and ~30 degrees linked end to end; or two rectangular sub-packings placed in opposite
corners of the box with the rest filled by uprights (a "composition": known records at this size are often built that
way).  What the parent learned: literal rectangular lattice blocks do not interlock with the uprights (the optimiser
sends them back to the grid); rows of a block must be shifted so its edges are staircases that meet the upright rows;
starts must be near the target side with small overlaps.  With --fill you only place the tilted squares.  Study
runs/evolve2/near/ for n = 234-240 and pick the n where a two-angle or composed structure appears.
