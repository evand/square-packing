## Reading

The proof shape is now clear on the verification side: a `t = 4` certificate is verified by
subdivision with `ADM` at the walls and `CHAIN` in the interior, with cliques and regions credited
per region rather than per cell.  On the family side, cliques + anchor pentagons are the
certifiable closure of local reasoning on a fixed support, and the pure instance sits ~0.1–0.2
below 12 on the lattice with a descending, non-terminating packing-side sequence.  Whether the
continuum values are below 12 is a cover-side question and can now be asked: run the `t = 4`
cover loop with the disjunctive checker as oracle, first on corner leaves `k <= 3` (a unit of
room), then `k = 4` and the pure instance.

## Decisions

- V1 proper = `zeromargin.py`'s `ADM + CHAIN + clip_bin` extended to clique pieces and region
  boxes with per-region crediting (`RUNG2.md` §9.4); the Rust sweep verifier stays a `t < 4` tool.
- Before any publication of `s(13) = 4`: an independent re-implementation of the checker
  (`xcheck`-style, ideally in Rust) and Lean statements for Lemmas A, E–H, `clip_bin`.
- The E1/E2 packing-side runs are done as instruments; no more lattice pricing for its own sake.
