## Reading

`s(13) = 4` case-free now has the trust base the `s(12)` bound has — two exhaustive checkers
sharing nothing, rejection tests, and Lean for every soundness lemma — and is ready to write up.

For `s(12)` the picture inverted again, and this time it is a measurement, not a plan: the
packing-side campaign since 09-08 measured a relaxation no certificate can express.  The
certifiable `t = 4` family — points, odd polygons (positive-volume membership: contains a side),
region boxes, chord — does not go below 12 at the corner leaf (exactly 12, polygons worth 0
there) or on the pure instance (≥ 12.15).  The sub-12 numbers lived on grazing tangencies: fan
cliques that are non-Helly, whose members meet with deficits of `-0.0007…-0.04`, so no
positive-volume region of pose space is contained in one.  V1 has no certificate to verify and
comes off the front of the path.  The research question is now sharp: **a certifiable object with
positive-volume membership that captures the fan mass — or a proof that none exists**, e.g. that
every pairwise-intersecting family of closed unit squares at `t = 4` of positive measure in pose
space has a common point (a Helly-type statement for squares, which would make cliques worth
exactly what points are worth and end the cover route at ≥ 12).
