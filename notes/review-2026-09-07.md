**The `t = 4` screen** (`search/T4SCREEN.md`, packing side, closed semantics, corner + per-slot
branch + wall and interior anchor cliques, calibrated against level2-design §4.4 exactly and against
the cover side at 3.98 by weak duality): pure 12.163 (certified); corner leaf `k = 4` pure

frame-free interior LP, whose threshold 4 decides an `m = 4` leaf, read 4.0 "converged" and climbed
to 7.53 under pricing — the pose sets carry ≥ 3.5 of slack on the governing quantity against leaf
deficits of 0.4–1.0.  Two structural facts: every `m = 4` leaf contains the 4×4 grid minus one slot
square per wall (nothing is refutable by capacity; the LP has to beat the grid itself, which is
infeasible only by strictness); and the packing optimum parks up to a unit of mass exactly on the
mid-wall slot boundary.

**Reading.**  The relaxation family is a good bounding machine and probably not sufficient at
`t = 4` on its own; what closes such leaves in the literature is deeper case analysis on the interior
squares with analytic strictness lemmas (chord lemma, capacities) as extra valid inequalities —
branch-and-bound over region assignments with LP bounds, Bentz's structure run by machine.  Nothing
today argues for a different family; nothing argues this one is enough.
