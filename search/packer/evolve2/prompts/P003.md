# P003 (gen 0): two bulbs
Parent: none.  Targets: reproduce n = 132, 182; hunt n = 133, 176, 183.

Structure (study refs 132, 175, 182, and 181 for the transition): two separate compact blocks ("bulbs") of tilted
squares, each a small rotated lattice (roughly 3-4 squares across by 4-8 along), at similar tilts (~25-35 degrees) but
in different places: typically one bulb against the left edge in the upper half and one against the bottom edge on the
right, each in its own region of the box, with a full axis-aligned grid between and around them.  The grid rows /
columns next to each bulb are shifted by fractional amounts to interlock with it; the two bulbs together absorb the
"missing" area that makes s < ceil(sqrt n).  Design a constructor that, given n, picks the box side, the two bulb
sizes, angles and positions (which edges / corners), and fills the rest with interlocking uprights.
