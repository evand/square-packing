# Final5Hi in Lean (2026-10-21)

`final5_high : Final5Hi` (`lean/Sqpack/Rect5Final.lean`): the real inequality left by the chain for five unit
squares in a strip of height 2√2 − 1 ≤ H < 2 (low strips: `rect5_low`, four midline points).  With
`rect5_strip_lower_hi_of` (Rect5.lean) it gives the strip part of R₅.

## Pipeline (`hi5.py`, reusing `../r5/cert5.py`, `gen5.py`, `tight5.py`)
* Dispatcher (`Rect5Final.lean`): half-angle coordinates u, v of both separating lines (`half_angle`), then
  2 × 2 slope signs × 2 × 2 one-square branches × 4 middle-square candidates = 64 case theorems
  (`Rect5HiCases{FF,FT,TF,TT}.lean`, generated by `gen5.emit_case`).  Each case splits on the sign of the
  middle square's slope μ (floor / ceiling) and of the kink |cr| (aligned candidates), reduces the hypotheses to
  "Lb ≤ L" (`chain5`), and splits H at h₀ = (6√2 − 1)/4: target 5 on [2√2 − 1, h₀], 4 + 6√2 − 4H on [h₀, 2].
* Leaves: 160 distinct polynomial inequalities in (u, v, H, r), r a variable with r ∈ [1.414213, 1.414214].
  * 144 non-tight leaves are linear in H: each is proved from its two H-endpoint polynomials (u, v, r only),
    with edge factors (u, 1 − u, 1 + u², …) stripped, by a Handelman certificate (float LP over products of
    the box / sign-region generators, `cert5.certify_split`; Lean's `linarith` finds exact coefficients from
    the product hints).  212 distinct endpoint cores, all certified, each LP < 5 s.
    Degenerate sign regions: 48 cores have opposite region generators v − u ≥ 0, u − v ≥ 0 (region u = v):
    rewritten to u = v and certified in one variable; 2 leaves have (u + v)(uv − 1) ≥ 0 (only the box corners,
    where the leaf vanishes): proved by hand (`emit_corner_leaf`).  2 cores needed merged LP supports (several
    LP solutions, degrees 6/8/10, random costs; `ROBUST` in hi5.py) because the float LP's support was not
    exactly feasible for `linarith`.  14 cores split at u or v = 1/2; the rest are one box.
  * 16 tight leaves (the zigzag, equality at u = v = √2 − 1 on the whole range h₀ ≤ H < 2): `tight5.emit_tight`,
    the decomposition (1 + u)ᵐ G = R + k Q at H = 2 with R a positive multiple of (u + 1 − r)² (exact, r² = 2),
    k the kink factor, Q of one sign in a band of u (certificate), plus certificates outside the band.
* Bugs fixed on the way (gen5's case-theorem text had never compiled): `hC10` → `hC₁0`; `field_simp` needs
  `1 − w ≠ 0`, `1 − w² ≠ 0` in the frontier branch (injected after `half_lt_one`); `positive_den`'s `mul_pos`
  chain did not match sympy's reordered product (now type-free factor proofs); and `cert5.parts` produced Python floats for the upright candidate ((1 + 0)/2); `hi5.py`
  rationalises (`_parts_exact`).  The old 10-21 run's leaves for that candidate had float coefficients.

## Why it now closes (the 10-21 attempt stalled at 120/160)
* H ≥ 2√2 − 1 removes the low-H part of the 'lo' leaves.
* The H-endpoint reduction removes H from every LP (two variables + r) and lets edge factors be stripped exactly.
