# P001 (gen 0): distorted strip
Parent: none.  Targets: reproduce n = 178, 207; hunt n = 177, 206, 208.

Structure (study refs 178, 207, 206, 208): a strip of tilted squares two squares wide runs roughly corner to corner
along the box diagonal, at a tilt near 40 degrees (not 45).  It is not one straight lattice strip: it is broken into
short segments (a few square-pairs each), and each segment is offset from the previous one by a step, so the strip
"staircases" along the diagonal.  The axis-aligned squares on both sides form two staircase-edged grid regions whose
rows / columns end against the strip; the step positions are where a grid row or column ends.  The strip's segment
lengths and step pattern are the design parameters; the record values sit at side k + ~0.85-0.92 for k = 13, 14.
Design a constructor that, given n, chooses the box side, the strip angle, the segment lengths and steps, and fills
the two staircase regions with uprights.
