## The promising idea, and its caveat (→ `tasks/leafa-proof`)

Bentz's replacement lemma (Stromquist's trick) fails at 12 boxes because there is no singleton *corner*
box; but in leaf A the eight wall/interior boxes are singletons, so it applies to them and yields forced
polygons `H_{c_j}, H_{d_j}` (computable exactly).  Caveat found on inspection: the lemma is degree-1, so no
two hulls can share a point — the certified measure has coverage `≤ 1` — and the hulls cannot kill the leaf
by double coverage.  What they do is give the pairwise chain argument explicit bookkeeping, which is how
Bentz uses them.  The task is therefore "hulls, then a written chain proof", with the `n = 11` rank-10
statement (margin-positive) as the rehearsal.
